# Intake — 8 questions, ~6 minutes

Run this **before** grading. The same page is good or bad depending on the offer, the
traffic and the audience; without these answers a verdict is decoration.

Order carries weight: **traffic → offer → audience**, and inside positioning
**the alternative before the differentiator.** Asking "what makes you different?"
first produces self-flattering answers, so Q6 comes before Q7.

Open with the budget so it does not feel like an interrogation:
> "Ocho preguntas, unos seis minutos, y luego te muestro la página."

## The eight

| # | Ask it like this | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | "En el último mes, ¿de dónde llegó la mayoría — alguien buscando en Google, un anuncio que pagas, Facebook o Instagram, recomendación, o tu propia lista de correo o WhatsApp?" | Traffic temperature and awareness stage; which benchmark applies at all |
| Q2 | *(ads/search only)* "Mándame el anuncio que traes corriendo y las cinco palabras que más llamadas te traen." | Ad-to-page message match |
| Q3 | "¿Qué recibe exactamente la persona, cuánto cuesta, y en cuánto tiempo lo tiene?" | The offer; feeds D-03 and the price signal |
| Q4 | "¿Qué tiene que hacer o ceder para empezar?" | Perceived effort; justifies every form field |
| Q5 | "¿Qué quieres que haga — llamar, agendar, llenar un formulario, ir al local? ¿Y qué es lo que de hecho pasa hoy?" | The goal. **The gap between wanted, actual, and most prominent on the page is itself a finding.** |
| Q6 | "Si esa semana hubieras estado llenísimo, ¿qué habría hecho ese cliente en vez de contratarte?" | The real alternative. Accept "lo hace él mismo", "lo deja para después", "el señor que le recomendó su vecino" — the status quo is the usual competitor |
| Q7 | "Piensa en los últimos tres clientes que clonarías. ¿Qué tenían en común?" | Audience; feeds J-05 leaf 2 |
| Q8 | "¿Qué es lo último que dudan justo antes de decir sí — y qué les contestas?" | **The verbatim rebuttal is usually the missing copy.** Feeds J-05 leaf 4 |

In SPEC mode, Q5 drops "qué es lo que de hecho pasa hoy" and becomes:
"Cuando alguien te llama o te escribe hoy, ¿qué es lo primero que hace?"

## Never ask — the answer is on the page

CTA presence, position, count or wording · click-to-call · field count and required
flags · price presence · testimonial presence, dates or specificity · star ratings ·
guarantee text · service area · hours · outbound link count · page weight and speed ·
mobile layout · contrast · labels and alt text · **tracking tags** · stock vs real
photography · reading level.

Asking any of these is a **report defect.** It costs credibility and it displaces a
question only the owner can answer. Before delivering, check every question you asked
against this list; any hit is a defect.

## What a missing answer blocks

| Missing | Consequence — state it, do not impute |
|---|---|
| Q1 | No benchmark applies. Do not compare to any industry median. |
| Q3 | D-03 cannot be judged: no price is known to be disclosable. |
| Q5 | M-02's attention ratio is undefined — a ratio needs a goal count. |
| Q8 | J-05 leaf 4 is unscorable. Report the headline score out of 4, and say so. |

Never invent an answer to keep the report tidy. A `NO MEDIDO` row is worth more than
a confident guess, because the reader can act on the first and not on the second.

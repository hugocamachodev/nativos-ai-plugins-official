# Forbidden numbers — the blacklist

Every figure that goes in a report or a recommendation passes this table first. The
left column must never be repeated; the right column is what survives.

Meta-rule: **one fabricated number found by the client discredits the other fifty,
including the true ones.**

| Never repeat | Why it fails | What survives |
|---|---|---|
| "60% nunca pasa del fold" | False. ~71% of visitors scroll; 66% of attention is spent below the fold (Chartbeat, 2bn visits). The 57% figure is viewing-*time* distribution, not people. | *The hero decides the visit. Attention is front-loaded.* No number. |
| "Tienes 3 segundos" (or 5, or 8) | Folklore. The only sourced comprehension window is **10 seconds** (NN/g over 2bn dwell times). The 5-second test's "5" is a test-administration convenience from a 2007 practitioner article with no cited study. | *Clear at a glance, and provably clear in a 5-second test.* |
| "50ms para decidir si se queda" | Lindgaard 2006 measured **aesthetic-appeal ratings** of whole screenshots, not a departure deadline and not conversion. The quote also truncates its own conditional, "*if you give people more time*". | *A visual first impression forms almost instantly.* Scope it to impression. |
| "El promedio convierte 2–3%" | Scoping error: 2.26% is **sitewide ecommerce purchase** conversion. Different numerator and denominator. Teaching it inflates the apparent effect of every technique 2–3×. | *Benchmark against your own industry median and traffic source.* No universal baseline exists. |
| "6.6% es la mediana de la industria" | Vendor-conflicted: Unbounce measuring pages built on Unbounce, self-selected customers who already bought a CRO tool, "conversion" = whatever goal the owner defined. | Present as *"clientes de Unbounce, metas autodefinidas."* |
| "15–25% es alcanzable" | Real but unremarkable at the top of the distribution — email-sourced landing pages average 19.3%. | *Channel explains more of the spread than page craft does.* |
| "Menos campos siempre convierte mejor" | Zuko: **739 forms, 93M sessions — the field-count trendline is flat.** | *Field TYPE is what is measured:* never a password, never a textarea, never stacked selects. |
| "Naranja/rojo/verde convierte mejor" | No traceable primary source for any button colour. | *Contrast:* 3:1 for the button, 4.5:1 for its label. |
| "Móvil convierte peor" | Reverses by vertical: legal 21% mobile vs 15.9% desktop; finance 11.5% vs 9%. | *Decide per vertical, with that vertical's data.* |
| "53% abandona tras 3 segundos" | Traceable only to an Akamai footnote citing "Google Data, n=3,700 **opt-in** sites", 2016 — and the original page 404s. | Cite it with that provenance, or not at all. |
| "70% de abandono de carrito" | Not a measurement: an arithmetic mean of 50 heterogeneous third-party studies, several of them vendors'. And it is ecommerce **checkout**. | Transferable as a *ranking of objections*, never as a rate for a lead form. |
| "1–2% llega al slide 2 del carrusel" | Conflates total carousel interaction with later-slide reach. The real figure is ~4% of a ~1% click rate ≈ **0.04% of visitors per later slide** — far worse than stated. | *Later slides are effectively invisible.* Scope: .edu homepages, 2012–13. |
| "Cinco reseñas = +270% de probabilidad de compra" | Vendor-conflicted and undisclosed: data "supplied by PowerReviews", a review-software vendor. No sample size, no product count, no methodology; the 2017 report 404s at the linked path. | Keep the *mechanism* — a count threshold, rating honesty, recency, verifiability. Drop the multipliers. |
| "El cerebro procesa imágenes 60,000× más rápido que el texto" | Internet folklore with no primary source. | *Decorative and stock imagery is skipped; real photos of real people are not.* |
| "Menos opciones siempre gana / el estudio de las mermeladas" | **Direction inverted.** 60% stopped at the 24-jam display vs 40% at the 6-jam one; purchase reversed (30% vs 3%). And the choice-overload meta-analysis (63 conditions, 50 experiments, N=5,036) found **D=0.02**, CI crossing zero. | *Variety attracts; simplicity converts.* Two jobs, two positions on the page. |
| "La gente compra con emoción, no con lógica" | Overstated as an absolute. Ju & Jang (n=311): the **rational** appeal had the greater positive influence on booking intention. | *Problem-Agitate-Solution works — but only paired with efficacy in the same block.* The fear-appeal meta-analysis (127 articles, 248 samples, N=27,372, d=0.29) finds effectiveness rises with efficacy statements. |
| "Un estudio reciente de Harvard encontró que…" as a credibility device | FTC Substantiation Notice enumerates **misrepresenting the level or type of substantiation as a separate offense.** You are exposed whether or not the underlying figure is true. | *Credibility = who you are, what you have done, and a source the visitor can open.* No URL, no number. |
| "Google Guaranteed: te devuelven hasta el costo del trabajo" | **Discontinued** for services booked after 7 Dec 2025; the badge is now "Google Verified". | Saying the old line on a live page is a false claim. Check the current badge. |
| "FID < 100ms" | **FID was retired in March 2024.** | INP, good ≤200ms. |
| "LCP < 1s", "TTFB < 200ms" as targets | House conventions 2–4× stricter than the standard, presented as targets. Failing a page against them fails pages Google rates "good". | Google's thresholds: **LCP ≤2.5s**, **INP ≤200ms**, **CLS ≤0.1**, TTFB ≤800ms. |
| "4–8 value props", "2–4 social proof sections" | Agency convention. Searched NN/g, Baymard, Unbounce, MECLABS, CXL, GoodUI and the peer-reviewed HCI literature: **no study establishes any of these counts.** GoodUI's 141 tested patterns contain no section-count pattern at all. | *One section per distinct offer facet or named objection you have evidence for*, traced to an intake answer. Checkable in minutes; a range never is. |
| "130,000 fijaciones" cited as sample strength | **n = 120 participants.** The impressive number is fixations, not people. | Cite as *"120 participantes, 130,000+ fijaciones."* |

## When a number is not on this list

It still needs a tier before it ships: a primary source with n and year, a
large-n-but-correlational figure with its conflict named, a convention labelled as
convention, or a regulation cited by section. If you cannot place it, leave it out.
